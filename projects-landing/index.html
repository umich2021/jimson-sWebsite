Soundboard.app

SoundBoard Caller

A soundboard for phone calls — tap a tile to drop a meme sound effect into whatever call you're on. Originally an Android app, now also a web version.

Favorites, a quiet/loud toggle, and an optional "bot mode" that injects sounds into a live conference call via a Twilio number.

Storage.txt

Twenty Gigabytes, Thirteen Cents

A small CLI that syncs a couple of folders to Backblaze B2, encrypted, so I could stop paying for Google and Dropbox storage I barely used.

A write-up on why I built it, the two-tier sync model, the cost math, and how it's put together.
