Storage.txt

I didn't want to pay google and dropbox $2 for storage

Problem: I was running out of storage on google and my computer, most of it was backups of photos/videos and archived files. So instead of paying money for a cloud storage I wanted to be on the free plan of google and dropbox, I found there was a dev friendly storage called Backblaze that allowed you to pay for storage per GB. For my needs I would've payed $.13 a month.

Using claude to recreate dropping and syncing files, I'm saving $3 a month.

Attached is a write-up on why I built it, the two-tier sync model, a rudimentary cost analysis, and how it's put together. Read the write-up
