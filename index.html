Aventos

Aventos is an AI anime studio — short-form, AI-generated anime built end to end, from concept and screenwriting to voice, animation, and post. The bet: anime-style storytelling doesn't just work as entertainment, it works as a marketing channel for B2B SaaS companies who all sound and look the same.

Platform

We turned Aventos's internal AI-video tooling — script-to-render pipeline, batch generation, asset management — into a creative-suite platform for other studios. The first paying customer was our own production team, buying generation credits to prove it out before opening it up further.

We also landed a publisher deal for "My Cat Is a Vegetarian" and a distribution deal with BlueBox.tv to get the content out further.

Project GEO

An AEO (answer-engine optimization) tool — it tracks how often and how well a brand shows up when people ask ChatGPT, Claude, and other AI assistants about brand discoverability. Built for teams who want to know if they exist in the AI-answer layer, not just search.

Stack

Solo build, top to bottom — the crawling/tracking pipeline that watches what AI answer engines cite, plus the product and dashboard on top. No cofounding engineer; I wrote the stack myself.

Traction

Took it from 0 to 1 — first paying B2B SaaS customers, sales run solo alongside the build.
